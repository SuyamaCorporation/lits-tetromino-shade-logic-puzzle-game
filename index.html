LITS Puzzle

Shade 4 connected cells per region to form tetrominoes — no 2×2 shaded blocks, identical touching tetrominoes forbidden

Rules

  • • Shade exactly 4 cells in each region
  • • Shaded cells in each region must be connected
  • • Shaded cells must form one of the 4 LITS tetrominoes: L, I, T, S
  • • No 2×2 block of shaded cells anywhere
  • • Two identical tetromino shapes cannot touch (even diagonally) — different shapes OK
